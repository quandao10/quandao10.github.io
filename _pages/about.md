I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Rutgers University, advised by <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=a7VNhCIAAAAJ&hl=en'>Distinguished Professor Dimitris Metaxas</a>. My research is on **efficient generative modeling**, mainly **diffusion and flow-based models**. I work on learning principles, training methods, and architectures that make these models more scalable and improve their sample quality while lowering the cost of training and inference. My central goal is high-quality generation with minimal compute and only a few function evaluations (NFEs).

During my Ph.D., I have been a research intern at **Adobe Research** (2026), developing perceiver transformers for pixel-space video diffusion, and at **Apple Machine Learning Research** (2025), training image diffusion models efficiently in the DCT frequency domain. Before Rutgers, I spent two years as a Research Resident at <a href='https://www.vinai.io/'>Qualcomm AI Research Vietnam</a> (formerly VinAI Research), working with <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FYZ5ODQAAAAJ&hl=en'>Dr. Anh Tran</a>. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from Monash University.

My work has been published at CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICLR, AAAI, and WACV.
